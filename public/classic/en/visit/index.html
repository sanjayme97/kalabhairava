Guidelines for devotees

  • Please leave footwear outside the temple.
  • Wear modest, traditional clothing.
  • Ask the priest before photographing the sanctum.
  • Keep the premises clean and avoid plastic.
  • Please do not touch the ancient inscription stone or apply anything to it.
