The deity

Kalabhairava, lord even of time

Kalabhairava is the fierce form of Lord Shiva. Kāla means time, and Kalabhairava is the master of time itself. He is worshipped as the kshetrapala — the guardian of the sacred place and of the village.

Holding the trishula and damaru, with the dog as his vehicle, Bhairava is believed to drive away fear and protect those who seek him.

The festival of the year

Sri Kalabhairaveshwara Jatra Mahotsava

On jatra day the whole village gathers. By day, devotees pull the chariot decked with coloured cloth, flowers and coconuts, and toss bananas towards it as an offering.

By night, in front of the illuminated temple, hundreds of devotees carry blazing aarati lamps on their heads — a river of light flowing down the village street.

Village heritage

A 900-year-old stone inscription

A Hoysala-period inscription dated 1101 CE is preserved at our temple. It is not about this temple — it tells of the founders of “Managatur”, the temples of that time and the gifts made to them.
