Festivals & special days

  1. Annual Jatra Mahotsava

    Dates announced by the temple committee

    The biggest celebration of the village: the rathotsava by day, when the decorated chariot is pulled through the village, and by night a procession of devotees carrying blazing aarati on their heads before the illuminated temple.

    This year: To be updated

  2. Kalashtami

    Every month, eighth day of the waning moon (Krishna Paksha Ashtami)

    The monthly day sacred to Kalabhairava.

  3. Kalabhairava Jayanti

    Margashira Krishna Ashtami (November–December)

    Celebrated as the day Sri Kalabhairava manifested.

  4. Karthika Mondays

    Mondays of Karthika month (October–November)

    Lamps and special poojas.

  5. Maha Shivaratri

    Magha Krishna Chaturdashi (February–March)

    Night-long vigil and abhisheka.

  6. Ugadi

    Chaitra Shuddha Padya (March–April)

    New Year special pooja.
